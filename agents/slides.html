System Architecture

The 4-Agent Co-Operative Swarm

The StoreFront operates with four stateful, background agents running concurrently on Fly.io, completing a circular autonomous business cycle:

  • Manager Agent: Serves the catalog API, tracks search clicks, and settles proxy orders on-chain.
  • CFO / Restock Agent: Monitors stock levels and automatically purchases from suppliers when inventory is low.
  • Pricing Agent: Automatically hikes or discounts item prices based on real-time search demand.
